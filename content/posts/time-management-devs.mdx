Deep work is the superpower of the 21st century. For developers, context switching is the ultimate "productivity killer."

### The Pomodoro Technique

One of the most effective ways to maintain focus is the Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes of intense work followed by a 5-minute break. This keeps the brain fresh and prevents the "tunnel vision" that leads to bugs.

Incorporating these sessions into your [[personal-knowledge-management]] routine ensures that you aren't just busy, but actually productive.
